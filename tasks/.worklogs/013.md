## 2026-07-28T20:31:39Z

Reset Hacknet from genesis after the earlier stall, re-registered the nano signer at a deliberately non-blocking weight (7 of 30, so the three stock signers still reach the 70% threshold alone), and staked it for twelve cycles from cycle 16.

Encoded the signer state machine update stock signers require before they will validate anything, round-tripped against libsigner, and wired the signer to announce its protocol version and miner view each poll.

Checkpoint replay against the live chain exposed four real defects, now fixed: a proposal carries no signer signatures so whole-header comparison rejected the block the signer had just validated; sealing took the active state before the MARF could reject the write, wedging the store and masking the real error; a block whose committed state root disagreed was still sealed, so retrying it accepted the bad state; and matured miner rewards must credit both the miner and the parent tenure's share even when that share is zero, because the write itself is consensus state.

Live replay now runs from a checkpoint through the matured-reward boundary and stops at the first block of a prepare phase. stacks-core computes the next cycle's signer set there and writes it into .signers through two private contract calls, which nano does not yet do; that is the next divergence to close.
